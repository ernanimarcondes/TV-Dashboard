# TV-Dashboard

- Preserve the upstream attribution and licenses.
- Never commit personal configuration, channel choices, credentials, signing keys, captures or app/account data.
- Run `python tools/privacy_check.py` before publishing. Use `python build.py` for a generic build; `--personal` is local only.
- Preserve Original mode, watchdog, Wi-Fi ownership safeguards and system-package protections inherited from Free My TV.
- Do not change TV picture, sound, network or account settings as part of dashboard work.
- Keep credentials and signing keys out of source and downloadable public artifacts. Do not use real TV captures in public docs unless separately reviewed and authorized.
